# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

One sentence, at most 240 characters, pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable — a product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody on earth needs. Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23).

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the pitch to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`).
